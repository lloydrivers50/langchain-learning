# Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy ID:** AUP-001
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025
**Review Date:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** Head of IT

## Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of company technology resources including laptops, networks, email, and software.

## Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff who use company-provided technology.

## Policy

### 1. General Use

Company devices are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive bandwidth, or expose the company to security risks.

### 2. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on company systems:
- Downloading or installing unlicensed software
- Accessing or distributing illegal content
- Using company email for political campaigning or commercial solicitation
- Connecting personal USB storage devices without IT approval
- Disabling or circumventing security software including antivirus and firewalls
- Sharing login credentials with any other person, including colleagues

### 3. Email and Communication

All company email is subject to monitoring and archival. Employees must not use personal email accounts for company business. Sensitive data must not be sent via email without encryption. Phishing attempts must be reported to IT Security immediately.

### 4. Remote Working

Remote workers must use the company VPN at all times when accessing internal systems. Work must not be performed on public WiFi networks without VPN. Screens must be locked when devices are unattended. Company data must not be stored on personal devices.

### 5. Software Installation

Only software from the approved software catalogue may be installed. Requests for new software must be submitted to IT and approved by the employee's line manager. Open-source software must be reviewed by IT Security before installation.

## Monitoring

The company reserves the right to monitor all activity on company systems without prior notice. This includes email, web browsing, file access, and application usage.

## Violations

First violation: Written warning and mandatory security awareness training. Second violation: Suspension of system access pending review. Third violation: Disciplinary action up to and including termination.
