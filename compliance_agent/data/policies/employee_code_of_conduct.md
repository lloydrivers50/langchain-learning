# Employee Code of Conduct

**Policy ID:** COC-001
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025
**Review Date:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** Head of Human Resources

## Purpose

This policy sets the standards of behaviour expected of all individuals working for or on behalf of the company.

## Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, temporary staff, and volunteers regardless of seniority or location.

## Policy

### 1. Professional Behaviour

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and dignity at all times. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying based on any protected characteristic is strictly prohibited. This applies to all interactions including in-person, email, messaging platforms, and social media.

### 2. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must declare any personal, financial, or family interests that could conflict with their duties. Declarations must be made in writing to HR and the employee's line manager. Employees must not make business decisions where they have a personal interest without prior written approval from a Director.

### 3. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees must not accept gifts valued above GBP 50 from any external party. All gifts and hospitality must be declared in the Gifts Register within 5 business days. Employees must never offer or accept bribes or facilitation payments under any circumstances. This obligation extends to family members and close associates.

### 4. Confidentiality

Company information must not be disclosed to unauthorised parties. This obligation continues after employment ends. Employees must not discuss confidential matters in public places. Social media posts must not reference client names, project details, or internal company matters without approval from Communications.

### 5. Whistleblowing

The company encourages reporting of any suspected wrongdoing including fraud, safety violations, discrimination, or regulatory breaches. Reports can be made to the employee's line manager, HR, or the confidential whistleblowing hotline. The company guarantees protection from retaliation for reports made in good faith. Anonymous reports will be investigated to the extent possible.

### 6. Outside Employment

Employees must obtain written approval before taking on any outside employment or directorships. Approval will not be unreasonably withheld where there is no conflict of interest. Approved outside employment must not interfere with the employee's duties or performance.

### 7. Company Property

Company property must be used responsibly and returned upon leaving. This includes laptops, phones, access badges, and intellectual property. Wilful damage to or theft of company property will result in immediate disciplinary action and may be reported to the police.

## Acknowledgement

All employees must sign an acknowledgement of this policy within 14 days of joining. Annual reconfirmation is required during the performance review cycle.

## Violations

Violations will be dealt with under the company's disciplinary procedure. Serious breaches may constitute gross misconduct and result in summary dismissal. Criminal conduct will be reported to the appropriate authorities.
