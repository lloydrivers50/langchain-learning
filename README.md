# **LangChain Learning**

A public log of my hands‑on journey into LangChain, LangGraph, RAG, and the wider world of agent‑driven AI systems.

This isn’t a theory repo or a collection of half‑watched courses.  
It’s a place to build, break things, fix them, and document what I learn along the way.

## **What’s in here**

- Notebooks from LangChain Academy
- Experiments with agents, tools, memory, and workflows
- RAG pipelines and retrieval tests
- Early LangGraph workflows
- Notes, diagrams, and small prototypes
- Anything else that helps me get sharper

## **Why this exists**

I’m moving fast into AI engineering and wanted a single place to track the work.  
Keeping it public keeps me accountable and shows the progression from “learning the pieces” to “building full systems.”

## **How it’s organised**

Each course or topic gets its own folder.  
Inside: notebooks, exercises, notes, and small demos.

Simple. Clean. Easy to grow.

## **Where this is heading**

The end goal is to build real agentic systems — the kind that use LangGraph, RAG, and knowledge graphs together.  
This repo is the groundwork.
